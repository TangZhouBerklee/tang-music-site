Album Preview

Rooted in neo-soul, Tang’s upcoming album moves freely between jazz, funk, and soul. The songs began to take shape during a time of intense pressure for Tang. Anxiety, depression, and melancholy give the music a cool undertone, yet a sense of warmth surrounds the whole record.

The album is currently in pre-production. Stay tuned.
